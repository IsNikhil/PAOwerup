Data Analyst — Acme Analytics (New Orleans, LA · Hybrid)

About the role
Acme Analytics helps regional retailers make smarter decisions with data. We are looking for an entry-level Data Analyst to join our insights team. You will turn raw data into clear dashboards and recommendations for business stakeholders.

What you'll do
• Write SQL queries to extract, join and aggregate data from our PostgreSQL and Snowflake warehouses
• Build and maintain dashboards in Tableau or Power BI that track sales, inventory and customer KPIs
• Clean, validate and transform datasets using Python (Pandas, NumPy)
• Run A/B tests and statistical analysis to measure the impact of promotions
• Present findings and recommendations to non-technical stakeholders in clear, concise language
• Partner with data engineers to improve ETL pipelines and data quality

What we're looking for
• Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics or a related field
• Strong SQL skills are required
• Experience with Python for data analysis
• Experience with Tableau, Power BI or a similar data visualization tool
• Solid understanding of statistics and hypothesis testing
• Excellent communication skills and attention to detail
• Nice to have: experience with Snowflake, dbt or Airflow
• Nice to have: familiarity with Git and Agile teams
